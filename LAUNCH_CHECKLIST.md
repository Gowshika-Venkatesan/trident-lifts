# Trident Website Launch Checklist

## Information required from Trident

- Official company name exactly as registered
- Primary sales phone number
- WhatsApp number
- Service or breakdown phone number, if different
- Official email address
- Full Bengaluru office address and Google Maps link
- Areas and cities currently served
- Working hours and emergency support hours
- GST and company registration details, if they should be published

## Claims that need evidence before publishing

- Year the company was established
- Number of completed installations
- Number of lifts under maintenance
- Team or technician count
- Response-time commitment
- Warranty terms
- Certifications and licence details
- Manufacturing or technology partnerships

Provide scans or clear photographs of certificates before adding certification badges.

## Product information

For every lift category, provide:

- Capacity range
- Speed range
- Maximum stops or travel
- Drive and machine type
- Door options
- Pit and overhead requirements
- Power requirements
- Standard safety features
- Cabin finish options
- Warranty and maintenance options
- Product brochure or technical PDF

## Project portfolio

Select at least six completed projects and provide:

- Project or customer name
- Bengaluru location or neighbourhood
- Building type
- Lift type, capacity and stops
- Installation or modernization date
- Three to six high-resolution original photographs
- Short description of the requirement and solution
- Written customer approval to publish

## Trust content

- Customer logos with permission
- Genuine customer reviews
- Names, roles and companies of testimonial providers
- Google Business Profile link
- Photos of the Trident team, office, installation work and service vehicles

Do not use stock or AI-generated imagery as completed-project evidence.

## Enquiry connection

The website form expects `VITE_FORM_ENDPOINT` in the deployment environment. Connect it to one of:

- Trident's own API and CRM
- Zoho CRM
- HubSpot
- Formspree or Web3Forms
- A serverless email function

The endpoint must accept a JSON `POST` request.

## Domain and launch

1. Shortlist and purchase the final domain.
2. Create official email accounts on that domain.
3. Choose hosting such as Cloudflare Pages, Vercel or Netlify.
4. Connect the enquiry endpoint and test lead delivery.
5. Add the final domain to canonical, Open Graph and sitemap settings.
6. Add privacy policy, terms and cookie handling.
7. Connect Google Analytics and Google Search Console.
8. Add the business to Google Business Profile.
9. Test phone, WhatsApp, forms and maps on mobile.
10. Run performance, accessibility and SEO checks before publishing.
